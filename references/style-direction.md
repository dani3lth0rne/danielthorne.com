# Style Direction

## Source References
- **danielthorne.com** (current live site) — all-caps wordmark, white bg, vertical card grid, sparse nav, work-first
- **saltandstone.com** — full-width hero, warm off-white, card grid, no accent colors, premium/minimal

## Aesthetic: Quiet editorial minimal
Not cold minimal — warm, spacious, unhurried. The kind of restraint that signals confidence.

## Color
- Background: warm off-white — `#f5f4f0` or `#fafaf8` (not pure white)
- Text: near-black — `#111111` or `#1a1a1a`
- Secondary text: `#666` / `#777`
- No UI accent colors — let photography and design work carry all color
- Borders/dividers: very subtle, `#e8e6e1` or similar warm gray

## Typography
- All-caps wordmark/name with wide letter-spacing (`0.15em`–`0.2em`)
- Clean sans-serif — system stack or something like Inter / Helvetica Neue
- Medium weights (400/500) — avoid heavy bold
- Generous line-height (`1.6`–`1.75` for body)
- Restrained hierarchy — size difference subtle, not dramatic

## Layout
- Generous whitespace as intentional element
- Card-based grid for work/photography
- Column-constrained body (~720–960px) but photography can break out full-width
- Substantial gutters between cards

## Navigation
- Sparse, text-only
- Reflect the two-sided split: Professional / Personal (or Work / Life, etc.)

## Photography
- Full-bleed or near-full-bleed — let images breathe
- Minimal caption chrome

## Mood words
Confident. Unhurried. Warm restraint. Editorial. Substance over decoration.
